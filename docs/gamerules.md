# Game Rules
## Chain Format
5 total words - 1 word revealed upon game start

## Valid Chain
A valid chain has 5 words in which each word pair connects

Chain = [Watch, Dog, Water, Bottle, Brush]

WATCH + DOG<br>
DOG + WATER<br>
WATER + BOTTLE<br> 
BOTTLE + BRUSH<br>

## Scoring / Deciding Winner

In both games, the winner is the user that correctly guesses the entire word chain first. In the case that this doesn't happen, each gameplay setting has their different rules:

### Timed Gameplay
If the timer ends, the winner is the player with the most correctly guessed words.
If the players are tied in words guessed, the game will result in a draw.

### Unlimited Gameplay
If both players cannot guess the full word chain, a draw can be agreed upon. The game then results in a draw.

## Lobby Mechanics / Timing

Upon creating a room and joining, there will be no timer until a second player joins. 

### Players
Users cannot join a room nor participate in a game until they have at least one saved <i>VERIFIED</i> list to use<br>
Host Leaves (Pre-Game): Lobby is closed, remaining players are removed<br>
Non-Host Leaves (Pre-Game): Active timers are dropped, lobby is reopened and waiting for a second player.<br>
Player Leaves (Live-Game): Handle later, typically whoever leaves is granted a loss<br>

### Pre-Game

#### List Selection
Once a second player joins the lobby, the Pre-Game starts. A 60-second timer will begin. If a player does not lock in one of their custom lists before the 60-second timer ends, a random list from that player's available lists is automatically selected and locked in. 

#### Countdown
Once both users have their lists locked in, a 10-second countdown will start the game.

### Live-Game
Once the 10-second countdown ends, the Live-Game starts. The first word of the opposing user's selected chain is revealed.

#### Timed Gameplay
The game will be live until the room-specified timer ends or when a user correctly guesses the entire word chain.

#### Unlimited Gameplay
The game will be live until a user correctly guesses the entire word chain or a draw is agreed upon.

### Post-Game
Players are awarded their Win/Loss/Draw to their record

## Guessing
- Guesses are case-insensitive
- Exact guesses are required
- Users cannot skip down the chain
